Keep Your Joint Balance Healthy.

Every grooming task is a withdrawal. Every reset is a deposit. When balance is healthy you feel good and work well. When it's low, stiffness builds. Push into overdraft and pain affects your work — recovery costs more time, effort and “interest.”

The Former Physio's Mission

Why GroomerReset Creator Gets It (Without Being a Groomer)

Noreen MacMahon is a physiotherapy-trained injury prevention specialist who has spent years watching groomers at work. She saw highly skilled professionals turning up with aching wrists, stiff backs, and sore shoulders — no practical guidance on how to protect themselves. By observing real grooming days up close, she mapped exactly when and where joints were being overloaded: the reach across the tub, twisting with the dryer, the lift onto the table.

GroomerReset translates her physiotherapy training into 30–90 second resets and simple setup changes you can use yourself, between dogs, without changing the way you groom.
